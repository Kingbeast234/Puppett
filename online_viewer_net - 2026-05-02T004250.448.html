Physics & Mathematics
Biharmonic Plate Equation
∇⁴W = (ρh / D) · ω² W
W = displacement · ω = 2πf · ρ = density
h = thickness · D = flexural rigidity
Flexural Rigidity
D = Eh³ / [12(1 − ν²)]
E = Young's modulus · ν = Poisson ratio
Natural Frequency (Free Square Plate)
fmn ≈ (π / 2L²)(m² + n²)√(D / ρh)
m, n = mode numbers · L = side length
Pāṇini Frequency Assignment
Fij = F₀ + i·Δrow + j·Δcol
i = place of articulation (0 Velar → 4 Labial)
j = manner of articulation (0 vl.stop → 5 nasal)
Consonants: F₀=150 Hz, Δr=40, Δc=25
Vowels: F₀=700 Hz, Δ=80–120 Hz per step
